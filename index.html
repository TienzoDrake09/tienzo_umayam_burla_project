HOW TO PLAY

  1. Enemies spawn at the outer ring (C) and move toward your ship.
  2. Use the joystick to face the enemy's sector (1 to 8).
  3. Flip the AMMO switch to match the color: red, blue, yellow.
  4. Press CLOSE (A), MID (B), or FAR (C) to match the distance.
  5. Wrong sector, color, or range = miss (-5 score).
  6. An enemy reaching the center costs 1 hull.
Keyboard: WASD / Arrows = aim
1 2 3 = ammo   Z X C = close / mid / far
P or Esc = pause
